# Powerups and permanent progression

Engineering progression stores gold and purchased modifiers; each purchase must apply to the next run and reject insufficient funds. Exact price formula, refund rules, caps and original names are unverified.

Required research: Might, Armor, Max Health, Recovery, Cooldown, Area, Speed, Duration, Amount, MoveSpeed, Magnet, Luck, Growth, Greed, Curse, Revival, Reroll, Skip, Banish, Omni, Seal/Charm and version-specific additions. Catalogue is a research checklist, not an assertion of current availability.

Distinguish player persistent powerups from per-run passive levels. Verify additive versus multiplicative stacking through controlled stat changes; do not infer order by matching only one end value.
