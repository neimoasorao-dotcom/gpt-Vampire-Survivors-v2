# Evolution, union, gift and morph

Provisional golden recipe: Whip max level + Hollow Heart + eligible chest → Bloody Tear; evolved weapon replaces base slot. Critical/heal behaviour must be measured, and is not implied by a name change.

Rule schema: `{id,base:[{id,level}],passives:[{id,minLevel}],chest:{eligible,minSpawnTime,types},stages:{allow,deny,overrides},characterOverride,blacklist,output,consume,replaceSlot,kind}`. Different recipes can require passive max levels or multiple weapons; union consumes more than one input; gift and morph can have distinct slot effects. Preserve chest spawn eligibility rather than using pickup time alone.

Exact Mad Forest eligibility time, early-boss exceptions, special chest types and current update exceptions: **unknown**. Cross-check recipe, chest eligibility and time rules independently before reference pass.
