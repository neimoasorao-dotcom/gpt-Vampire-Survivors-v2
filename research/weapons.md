# Weapons and behaviour fidelity

Whip hypothesis (`estimated`): lateral melee sweep, first strike toward facing, higher levels add opposing/stacked strikes; pierces a group; affected by Might, Area, Cooldown and Amount. Provisional numeric baseline: 10 base damage, 1.35 s cooldown, eight base levels. These are memory-derived candidates requiring acquisition and measurement, not wiki-confirmed facts.

Weapon definition schema: damage/baseDamage, cooldown, area, speed, duration, amount, pierce, knockback, critChance/multiplier, hitbox, interval, poolLimit, lifetime, targeting, direction, spawnOffset, acceleration, rotation, bounce/reflection, areaTick, retaliation, heal, debuffs, statScaling, levelTable, evolution/union/gift/morph, exception rules and event modifiers.

Distinct behaviour research checklist: Whip melee; Magic Wand nearest-target homing/direct shots; Knife facing stream; Axe gravity arc; Cross outward/return path; King Bible orbit; Fire Wand slow aimed fireballs; Garlic aura with per-enemy hit cooldown; Santa Water falling persistent zones; Runetracer ricochet; Lightning Ring target-position AoE; Pentagram screen clear/drop deletion; birds rotating bombardment; Song of Mana vertical zone; paired pistols diagonal patterns. These descriptions are provisional behavioural hypotheses. Do not treat common-projectile parameter changes as full implementations.

Laboratory protocol: isolated 30-second sample, fixed HP/density/speed, seeded stationary and moving targets, record emitted attacks/projectiles, hits, damage, effective range and per-target distribution; test each level and stat independently.
